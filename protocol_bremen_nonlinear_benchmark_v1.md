# Bremen nonlinear comparison v1 — post-result, locally fixed

The fixed configuration used in Bolzano Jan–Apr 2026 is evaluated on Bremen March–December 2023 after the earlier Bremen outcomes and Bolzano nonlinear outcome have been observed. This is a post-result exploratory comparison, not an untouched test, public preregistration, independent analyst or new site. Four office room series from one dataset may be correlated; no population/site inference. Preserve earlier results.

## Inputs and fixed methods
Verify pinned raw Git blob and locally recorded SHA-256 hourly Bremen artifacts, the source IDEAL selection, 55 homes and 125,779 source rows. Read 4 preprocessed hourly UTC series, no imputation, 10–45 C and quality criteria per Bremen audit. Reuse exact monthly windows, six-hour future target, inputs, monthly selection rules and fallback of `protocol_bremen_fallback_v1.md`; observed targets and history strictly pre-month. Averaged current hour available only at its end, communications latency unmeasured. Rebuild all six existing method predictions and validate per-sensor, per-month opportunities, ready/scored counts and sum absolute errors, and monthly history and choices against the saved Bremen artifacts BEFORE fitting or scoring the new nonlinear method. Stop on mismatch. Check protocol hash and original prior outputs intact.

For each room and month fit the fixed scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor configuration from `protocol_bolzano_2026_benchmark_v1.md` on the previous 30 days of complete historical features and targets, requiring >=24, residual target target-minus-previous-day, predictors t,t−1,t−18h, loss squared_error, max_iter 100, learning_rate 0.1, max_leaf_nodes 15, min_samples_leaf 20, l2_regularization 1.0, early_stopping False, random_state 20261029, other parameters defaults. This is one untuned model setting and same inputs/history as local ridge, not state-of-the-art or a general nonlinear test; it is not added to selector candidates. Source ridge has prior IDEAL training, local methods and selector need target-room historical labels.

## Metrics
Primary support: each issue must have observed target plus all seven predictions. Compute MAE and RMSE for each room on exactly this shared support; aggregate means equally across the four rooms (in particular aggregate per-room RMSE, not pooled RMSE). Report per-room MAE/RMSE, support counts, monthly MAE/counts and for selector vs local ridge, source and boosting the four room-level difference directions (ties tolerance 1e-8) and number of months. Record model readiness on all issue opportunities, separately own-support outcomes, common paired selector/fallback vs persistence and branch counts; different denominators are not interchangeable. No statistical significance, independent-room/site confidence interval or climatic inference. Record issue-wise predictions for verification, versions, data/protocol hashes. Any additional comparison after observing this outcome must be clearly post-result. Protect old and new outputs from overwrite. Test history cutoff and common-support arithmetic; read-only verifier cannot establish independently retrained model or real sensor timing. No claims of savings, HVAC control, or deployment performance.
