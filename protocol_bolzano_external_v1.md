# Bolzano external evaluation and timestamp sensitivity v1

Locally frozen after previous IDEAL and Bremen results and Bolzano source/structure quality inspection, before computing Bolzano forecast errors. Not public preregistration or independent review. Dataset: Pozza 2026, Zenodo record 20098783, four source files and published MD5 as in `outputs/bolzano_quality_v1.json`; one residential room, not independent sites. 2023 is warmup only, evaluation 2024-01-01 through 2026-01-01 exclusive (24 calendar months); 2026 file is held aside from model fitting and evaluation. Its checksum may be verified. Unknown original timezone and DST require explicit scenarios; do not claim UTC for primary timestamps.

## Preprocessing
Verify all four source MD5 and local SHA256 against quality audit. Read only timestamp and Temperature from 2023–25, parse to timezone-naive datetimes and numeric Celsius; remove unparseable, missing, values outside inclusive 10–45 C. Combine rows, and for primary `nominal_mean` average exact duplicate timestamps before flooring times to five-minute bins; average within each distinct five-minute bin; form equal-bin mean hourly if >=8 of 12 distinct bins; no interpolation. This primary is a nominal equally spaced clock, NOT verified UTC and not necessarily real elapsed time across DST. Hourly label denotes start of bin and issue after its end; 6h means six bins, and target is a mean of a later hour, not instantaneous. Sensor latency unmeasured. Hourly preprocessing differs from IDEAL and Bremen.

Two prespecified secondary sensitivities: `nominal_drop_conflict` drops all rows at exact timestamp groups with >1 distinct valid temperature, averages remaining exact duplicate timestamps and repeats primary aggregation; `hypothetical_rome` treats raw naive times as Europe/Rome civil time, discards rows ambiguous at fall-back or nonexistent at spring-forward, converts to UTC, then averages duplicates and aggregates. This is a hypothetical clock interpretation, not a verified property of the dataset. Timezone conversion changes the hourly axis, monthly boundaries and included samples; compare summary outcomes descriptively, not as paired errors at identical physical times. Record removed rows and supports. Avoid timezone/feature-interpretation claims absent author verification. Do not invent offsets for primary or conflate sensitivity with certainty.

## Source and model
Train exactly the fixed IDEAL development-source daily-residual ridge on 55 source homes, 125,779 feature rows, target before 2017-09-01 UTC; verify cohort matches saved selection. For each month, history uses only complete target-plus-feature rows from previous 30 days whose target hour begins strictly before month start; require >=24 for selector and local ridge. Choose among persistence, previous-day and source ridge with history MAE, fixed tie order persistence/day/source. The inference route is selector (requires full feature set and eligible history), otherwise persistence (current-hour measurement), otherwise day-reference (t−18h), otherwise abstain. Source needs t,t−1,t−18h. Local ridge uses complete history, excluded from selector. Issue hours follow monthly windows with t+6h strictly inside same month, as Bremen. Historical data and inputs are all from same room; no weather, occupancy or target-year fitting for source. Recompute selection per scenario, not borrow information across clock assumptions.

Any missing outcomes remain missing and no estimates replace them.
