# Exploratory multi-horizon pilot

After inspection of previous pilots, horizons 1, 6, and 24 hours are evaluated on 2019–2021 NEST data; not a registered confirmatory experiment. Samples use issue-time observations only, predicting the indoor hourly mean h hours later, with no intervening planned HVAC, actual future control or future weather. This makes the setup an unconditional forecast under historical operation, not a control-conditioned model.

For each horizon target timestamps determine partitions. Source unit training ends before 2020-11-01; target room adaptation has 7 or 30 days starting November 1; calibration uses December; test is Jan–Mar and Apr–Jun 2021. Models include persistence, source ridge, ridge adapted toward source using target 7/30 days, and target-only ridge at 7/30 days. Features and ridge settings match the prior pilot. Source transformations are estimated from source data, target-only transformations from target adaptation. All methods use identical eligible complete-case data for each room, horizon and season. Target and current-hour averages must have at least 45 observed minutes. Different horizons may select different records; do not compare metrics across horizons as if samples were identical.

The same December calibration yields nominal 90% absolute-residual intervals separately for each method, room and horizon. Season dependence and serial correlation preclude any claimed unconditional finite-sample guarantee. Metrics are MAE, RMSE, mean interval width, and observed coverage. Source/target rooms share the same geographic site, weather station, and infrastructure. No geographic generalization, real outage resilience, control or energy-saving claim follows. All metrics are exploratory; hyperparameters were not tuned and periods were chosen after prior pilots.

Correction after discovery: calibration input-hour start must be at or after December 1, after the longest target adaptation window; test input-hour start must be at or after January 1 when calibration has ended. Target timestamps also remain in their respective periods. Pre-correction results are archived, not publication-ready.
