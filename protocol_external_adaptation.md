# External target adaptation: exploratory protocol

Written before running this study, but after inspecting previous source-only PLEIA results; exploratory and not preregistered. One source site (NEST), one target site (PLEIAData). Target data from 60 eligible sensors are clustered within rooms/one site; per-sensor metrics do not imply independent buildings or statistical significance.

Source: all three NEST units, target timestamp < 2020-11-01; temperature-only predictors and source model as protocol_external_pleia.md. PLEIA raw offsets are converted to UTC; no uncertain weather clock is used. Data processing, 15–40°C filter and observed-hours rule as protocol_external_pleia.md. No future values are used for inputs. Adaptation uses target timestamp 2021-01-01 inclusive to Jan 8 exclusive (7 days) or Jan 31 exclusive (30 days); Feb 1–Mar 1 calibrates intervals separately per model and sensor; test target timestamps Mar 1–Dec 18 exclusive. Gaps retained between periods. Calibration uses target labels for all models including source zero-fit; distinguish point model zero-shot from interval calibration.

Compare seven point models: persistence; seasonal daily persistence (target's hour from previous day: issue-hour minus 24-h for horizon h, where h in {1,6,24}); source ridge; source ridge adapted towards source coefficients using 7 or 30 days; target-only ridge with 7 or 30 days. All methods use the same complete target cases including seasonal baseline. For h=24 the daily baseline and persistence are identical. Require 24 observations in fit and calibration; omit per-model results if insufficient, report counts. `fit` and `adapt` penalties are exploratory constants, not equal-compute or optimized; multiple candidate models and adaptation periods were selected after seeing earlier pilot results.

Source-only scaling is fit using source data. Target-only scaling uses only its adaptation period. Do not train or tune with calibration/test. Nominal 90% per-sensor symmetric residual intervals are calibrated in February and evaluated unchanged in March–December; no guarantee under temporal dependence/seasonal shift. Compute per-sensor mean absolute error, root mean squared error, empirical coverage and interval width. Summarize by horizon/model and by seasons March–May, June–August, September–November, December 1–17; equal-weight per sensor. Clustered samples, selection from only sensors with observed targets, naive source timestamp semantics, restrictive temperature bounds, 4 of 6 slots per hour and the one-location external test are material limitations.

Correction after discovery: calibration input-hour start must be at or after January 31, after the 30-day fitting period; test input-hour start must be at or after March 1 after calibration ends. Target timestamps must also remain inside their respective periods. Corrected four- and six-slot runs replace archived pre-correction outputs.
