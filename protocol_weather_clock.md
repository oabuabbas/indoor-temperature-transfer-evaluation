# Murcia weather clock diagnostic

Exploratory post-hoc diagnostic, not a validation of station time, not a forecast or proof of local/UTC status. Source raw PLEIA `MU62_dm.txt` (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7620136) and official station longitude -1.13471944° for MU62 from https://datosabiertos.carm.es/odata/Agricultura/IMIDA_estaciones.csv. The weather file has naive day-month-year date and clock strings; the station's encoding, observation interval label, timestamps, DST and any latency remain undocumented. Compare three hypotheses: naive clock means UTC, Europe/Madrid with daylight saving, or UTC+01 fixed year-round. For ambiguous/nonexistent local clocks exclude (do not infer offsets). This does not use indoor sensor or forecast results.

Select UTC days with at least 100 nonmissing ten-minute irradiance observations and maximum >=600 W/m2, then compute daily maximum-radiation timestamp; ties choose the earliest. Estimate solar noon using approximate equation-of-time `B=2π(n-81)/364; E=9.87 sin(2B)-7.53 cos(B)-1.5 sin(B)` minutes, longitude -1.13471944°, solar noon UTC minutes =720−4*longitude−E. Approximation is a consistency reference, not a weather timestamp or alignment validation. Clouds, choice of max vs true noon, period-end vs period-start timestamps, hour shifts, local obstructions and time zones can bias results. Report median signed and median absolute offsets, counts by winter/summer and excluded ambiguous/invalid timestamps for each hypothesis. Do not select an offset solely because it minimizes this difference; obtain official timestamp documentation or independent weather observations before reporting alignment as fact.
