# ThermBuild external temperature-only evaluation

Written before computing ThermBuild forecast errors, after examining archive structure and time metadata. Model family and evaluation were informed by earlier exploratory results on other datasets; this is not a prospectively registered or fully blind study.

Use the two *measured raw* TwinHouse files, not the simulated buildings or gap-filled measurements. The two houses are at one location and do not represent two independent climates. Use seven room air-temperature columns. No target-site fitting, calibration, tuning, or selection. Source data are the existing NEST units before 2020-11-01 (forecast target strictly before cutoff); pooling rooms as in `daily_residual.nest_source`.

TIME increments by one per record despite paper table describing hours. Published resolution is 15 minutes and full consecutive_days contain 96 rows. Assume these are ordered contiguous 15-minute observations and the start of each full day defines a common hourly grid; actual timezone, daylight-saving behavior and calendar alignment remain unverified. Retain complete 96-row days 2 through 443, require every expected day and the consecutive TIME sequence; otherwise fail rather than silently compress gaps. Aggregate each four ordered records by mean, requiring at least three valid temperatures (15–40 °C inclusive). Do not interpolate or use weather or clock time. The nominal hour ends at the last observation of its four-record group; issue predictions only after that observation. This defines a relative observation interval, not verified clock timestamps.

Forecast horizons 1, 6 (primary descriptive horizon), and 24 hours. Evaluate on the common valid rows for all four models: persistence, previous-day same relative hour, source ridge predicting deviation from that daily baseline, and source ridge predicting deviation from the current temperature. Both ridge models use the same three features: current temperature, previous hour, and previous-day reference aligned to target time (lag 24-h); source ridge penalty 100 and standardization fitted only to source. For h=24 the daily reference is current temperature. Compare per-room MAE/RMSE, equal-room per-house means and equal-house descriptive means; also counts and signed difference versus daily baseline. Do not treat correlated observations or rooms as independent sites; do not infer population significance from this site. All eligible observed periods are evaluation, with no target training. Reporting is conditional on the time assumption and physical plausibility filter; not a deployment or energy-saving claim.

Source: https://doi.org/10.24406/fordatis/445 and https://arxiv.org/html/2606.01994v2 . Raw archives retain their original CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Local SHA-256 records aid reproducibility but are not provider-verified checksums.
