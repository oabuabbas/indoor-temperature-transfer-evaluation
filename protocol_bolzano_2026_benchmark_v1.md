# Locally fixed 2026 Bolzano nonlinear benchmark v1

Fixed after examining Bolzano 2024–2025 forecast outcomes and quality metadata (including 2026 raw file quality), but BEFORE computing any 2026 forecast errors. This is not public preregistration, independent analyst replication or a wholly untouched scientific design. Four months from one room are temporally later than the prior evaluation, not four sites. The dataset clock timezone is unresolved. UTC is NOT asserted for primary; no latency, operational control or energy inference.

## Data and preprocessing
Verify raw 2025 and 2026 MD5 and SHA-256 against saved quality inventory; earlier dates are unnecessary because each history is at most 30 days. Use the `nominal_mean` hourly preprocessing in `protocol_bolzano_external_v1.md` (mean exact duplicate temperatures, floor to five-minute bins, average in bins, >=8 bins/hour, 10–45 C, no imputation), full sensor files for preprocessing, but examples must satisfy the following cutoffs. Primary issue windows are four calendar months, 2026-01-01 through 2026-05-01 exclusive. Within each month issue index t has t+6h strictly before next month. Time label denotes the start of the averaged hour, which is available only after it finishes. No outcome outside the test windows is evaluated or used for policy decisions. Secondary sensitivity `hypothetical_rome` localizes naive raw timestamps with ambiguous/nonexistent times discarded then converts to UTC; this is not verification of the true clock and changes samples/clock boundaries. Report both separately; no paired comparison across the different axes.

## Prespecified models and fair input support
Use fixed IDEAL source ridge trained on 55 source-development homes and 125,779 rows before 2017-09-01. At each month start use previous 30 days of complete feature + target examples, with target-hour start strictly before the month start, and >=24 examples. Fit local ridge using the same residual target `target - seasonal_yesterday` and same three features t,t−1,t−18h as before; also fit sklearn `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` to the same historical rows and residual target with `loss='squared_error', max_iter=100, learning_rate=0.1, max_leaf_nodes=15, min_samples_leaf=20, l2_regularization=1.0, early_stopping=False, random_state=20261029`; all other library defaults. Hyperparameters are fixed without tuning on 2026. No weather, occupancy, deep learning, GPU or extra predictors. Not a state-of-the-art claim. Source model has different prior training data; local models and selector use local labels.

Rolling selector remains unchanged: choose persistence/daily/source according to previous 30-day complete-feature historical MAE, tie order as existing; do not add boosting to selector candidates or tune selector on test data. Fallback is unchanged. Persistence, daily, source, local ridge, selector, fallback and nonlinear model issue under their usual readiness conditions, but primary comparison is all seven predictions plus observed target on identical support, with MAE and RMSE pooled by hourly opportunity. On this common support compare selector vs nonlinear and local ridge, and provide monthly MAE and counts for all methods. Separately show all-issue readiness, own-support scoring counts, fallback vs persistence paired support, chosen models, missingness and output availability; do not compare own-support MAE as if support were equal. Report results regardless of direction. 2026 months are correlated, no significance or population inference.

## Reproducibility and preservation
Store per-issue predictions, test month choice/history counts, per-method and monthly metrics, protocol and source-file hashes, software versions and primary/sensitivity summaries. Do not overwrite prior results. Verify common support, metrics, time cutoff and point routing, with tests covering history truncation and no target leakage. Validate preprocessing against previous 2025 hourly output on an inner common interval far enough from 2025 year boundary to avoid source-file differences; no comparison of actual 2026 errors until after this locally fixed plan. The resulting benchmark is one fixed configuration, not proof of best possible nonlinear or modern method. Single-room sensitivity, not generalization across buildings/climates; nominal timestamp issue remains unresolved.
