# IDEAL raw room-temperature audit, before forecast evaluation

Audit raw files in the official room-and-appliance archive whose names end `_room_temperature.csv.gz`, joined to the original `sensor`, `sensorbox`, and `room` metadata on sensor and home IDs. Do not use survey or person metadata. This is a data-quality inventory, not a locked final analysis plan or model test. Record archive size, provider MD5 and match before processing. Stream each individual ZIP member through gzip and pandas, without extracting the entire archive. Record parsing errors or malformed units, empty files, start/end UTC, bad timestamps, duplicates and nonmonotonic time, nonnumeric and implausible values and sensor position flags. Raw values are tenths °C; derive °C before filtering.

For a provisional quality screen, aggregate 10-minute UTC slots after excluding readings outside 15–40°C and duplicates. A slot qualifies if it contains at least 25 distinct valid timestamps; an hour qualifies when at least 4 of its six 10-minute slots qualify. This operational threshold is a study choice, not a provider-certified rule, and it does not prove all parts of a slot were observed. Count timestamps outside 2016-01-01 through 2018-12-31 UTC separately and exclude them from quality windows, avoiding an unrealistic grid for broken timestamps. Retain calendar gaps on a regular hourly index; no imputation. Count qualified hours, first/last, and the maximum number of qualified hours in any complete 60-day window within the series span (1440 hours), requiring at least 720 qualified hours and complete 60-day span for provisional coverage eligibility. Exclude sensor series with time reversals, no timestamps, inconsistent metadata identity, or processing errors from provisional eligibility. Duplicates are deduplicated; non-adjacent duplicates imply a time reversal and therefore exclude the series. Cross-chunk duplicate boundary must be handled. This threshold screens availability only; it does not establish forecasting eligibility with features, calibration splits, or guaranteed independence.

Report per-sensor and per-home aggregates; a home is counted as provisionally coverage-eligible if any one room sensor passes. Do not pick best model by these measures. Aggregate and report position flags separately rather than silently discarding flagged sensors; final exclusion rules must be frozen later. Study uses identifiers already anonymized by source and avoids displaying survey attributes. Resume successful sensor audits without repeating them; fail rather than treat a corrupted file as valid. All results are descriptive and have not assessed forecast accuracy, and IDEAL's dates predate current NEST training.
