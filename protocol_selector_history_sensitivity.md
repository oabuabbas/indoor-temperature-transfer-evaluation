# Exploratory selector history sensitivity, 6-hour forecasts

This follow-up was designed after observing prior PLEIA and ThermBuild selector outcomes; it is exploratory and is not independent or prospectively registered. Reuse the same source NEST daily-residual model and fixed candidates, hourly preprocessing, 30-day test-block boundaries, timestamp assumptions, target exclusion at test boundaries, and tie order as the existing site-specific protocols. Compare rolling selection from prior 7, 14, and 30 days. All choices are computed separately by sensor/room and frozen at the test-block boundary. Each validation set requires at least 24 valid rows with issue time inside the history interval and target strictly earlier than the block start. Test issue time is at or after start and target strictly earlier than end; use at least 24 rows. Exclude a sensor-block from **all** methods if any of the three histories fails eligibility so methods are evaluated on identical test rows. Fixed model candidates, source training, and decision policy are unchanged.

Report per sensor/room test MAE pooled across its eligible blocks, followed by equal-room within house and equal-house for ThermBuild, equal-sensor for PLEIA; report room/sensor counts, eligible sensor-blocks, prior-history sample counts and selected model frequencies. Within each site, report wins/losses versus 30 days per sensor/room and per block; ties distinguished. Compare both buildings and all PLEIA sensors only descriptively; their room counts, preprocessing and deployment periods differ. No site-population inference or formal significance claim. Selection uses previous target outcomes, needs measurement availability and does not examine operational overhead. Missingness or inadequate validation may affect applicability; selection of a history length after these results must be tested on untouched sites.
